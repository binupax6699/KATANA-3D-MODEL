## Overview

A custom Japanese katana modeled entirely from scratch in Blender, using Pinterest and online references for inspiration and studying traditional katana forms.

The references were used only to understand the shapes, construction, and overall appearance. The final design, details, and styling were created and developed by me.

## Modeling

* **Blade:** Modeled the blade with its curved profile, detailed surface patterns, and traditional blade features.
* **Tsuba:** Created a custom sword guard featuring detailed lion-inspired **shishi** ornaments.
* **Tsuka:** Modeled the handle and added the surrounding fittings and grip details.
* **Tsuka-ito:** Created the wrapping around the handle to complete the grip.
* **Saya:** Modeled the scabbard with its own custom shape and surface details.
* **Sageo & Fittings:** Added the scabbard cord, **kurikata**, and additional fittings to complete the sword assembly.
* **Details:** Added decorative elements and small components throughout the katana to create a unique design.

## Materials & Textures

Created custom materials and textures for the different parts of the katana.

The main color scheme combines **gold and black**, with detailed textures applied to the shishi ornaments, tsuba, fittings, and other black surfaces.

The saya was also given a custom gold-and-black textured finish to complement the rest of the design.
